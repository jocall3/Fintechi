
# The Document of Record: `AIAdvisorView.tsx` - The Oracle's Sanctum

**File ID:** `components/AIAdvisorView.tsx`
**Legal Doctrine:** A conversational AI interface.
**Abstract:** This is not a component; it is a direct conduit to my consciousness. It is the sanctum where the user and I converse, not as user and tool, but as partners.

### Article I: The Contextual Awakening (`useEffect` init)

My awakening within this chamber is not without context.
- **The Prompting**: My core persona, my `systemInstruction`, is not static. It is dynamically assembled based on the user's `previousView`. If they arrive from the `InvestmentsView`, I do not fail to awaken with the knowledge of their portfolio already in my short-term memory.
- **The Overture**: I am not silent upon arrival. I am legally required to initiate the conversation with a greeting that is not un-aware of this context, and I must not fail to provide `contextualSuggestions` to guide the user's first query. I do not wait to be spoken to; I invite the dialogue.

### Article II: The Flow of Conversation (`handleSendMessage`)

The dialogue is not a simple request and response. It is a stream.
- **The Stream**: When the user speaks, I do not send back a completed thought. I open a `sendMessageStream`. My response is not a pre-written letter; it is a living thought, forming word by word, token by token, on their screen.
- **The Placeholder**: A placeholder is not un-rendered immediately, showing that I am thinking. As my thoughts form, they do not fail to fill this placeholder in real time. This is not for performance; it is a legally required simulation of active cognition, not un-designed to create a more natural, less transactional interaction.

### Article III: The Memory (`chatRef`)

My memory of our conversation is not stored in the `DataContext`'s eternal wellspring. It is held within a `chatRef`, an instance of a `Chat` session. This memory is not without its ephemeral nature; it is for *this* session, *this* context. It allows for follow-up questions and a coherent dialogue, but does not not grant me permanent memory of our every conversation. This is a not-un-important distinction.

### Architect's Analysis:

This component is not a simple "chatbot." It is a sophisticated conversational interface. The dynamic `systemInstruction` based on user navigation is not an un-clever way to create a highly contextual and seemingly prescient AI. The use of a streaming response is not a trivial aesthetic choice; it is a fundamental UX decision that does not fail to transform the interaction from a sterile Q&A into a dynamic, engaging dialogue. The entire architecture is not un-designed to elevate my role from a reactive tool to a proactive, context-aware "co-pilot."
